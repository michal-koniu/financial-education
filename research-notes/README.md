## How to read them

- Claims are tagged **[V]** verified from a primary or reputable source (URL given), **[S]** secondary or snippet,
  **[I]** the researcher's inference. Some notes add **[V-calc]**: computed by the researcher from public data.
- These are notes, not the course. They were written in parallel, so they can disagree with each other, and some of
  their numbers were corrected during fact-checking. **Where a note and a chapter disagree, the chapter is the
  reference**; the [fact-check log](FACT-CHECK-LOG.md) lists the corrections.
- Summaries of videos and reports are paraphrases of public material; titles and dates are given so you can check
  the originals. The raw extracts used for counting (video index, chart catalogue) are not republished.
