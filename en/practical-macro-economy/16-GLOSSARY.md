## 0–9

- **2-year Treasury yield** (DGS2, TVC:US02Y) — The yield on 2-year US government debt. It tracks where markets expect the policy rate to average over the next two years, so it usually moves before the Fed does. → [03 · Short end: 3-month bill and 2-year yield (DGS3MO, DGS2)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#short-end-3-month-bill-and-2-year-yield-dgs3mo-dgs2); [02 · Fed funds versus the 2-year yield ("the Fed chases the 2-year")](02-THE-FED-AND-INTEREST-RATES.md#fed-funds-versus-the-2-year-yield-the-fed-chases-the-2-year)
- **3-day close** — The closing price of a three-day candle, used like weekly closes to confirm a break with less noise than daily candles. → [10 · Weekly, monthly, daily and 3-day closes](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#weekly-monthly-daily-and-3-day-closes)
- **3-month Treasury bill** (DGS3MO, TB3MS, TVC:US03MO) — The yield on 3-month US Treasury bills; it sits very close to the policy rate and anchors the 10y–3m spread. → [03 · Short end: 3-month bill and 2-year yield (DGS3MO, DGS2)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#short-end-3-month-bill-and-2-year-yield-dgs3mo-dgs2)
- **5y5y forward inflation expectation** (T5YIFR) — Market-implied average inflation for the five years starting five years from now, from Treasuries and TIPS. The Fed's preferred check that long-run expectations stay anchored. → [03 · Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) and the 5y5y forward (T5YIFR)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#breakeven-inflation-t10yie-and-the-5y5y-forward-t5yifr); [04 · Inflation expectations: Michigan surveys and market breakevens](04-INFLATION.md#inflation-expectations-michigan-surveys-and-market-breakevens)
- **8-week SMA** (8W) — A short weekly moving average used as a fast trend line on bitcoin charts. → [10 · 200-day SMA (and the 50-day, 100-day and 8-week)](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#200-day-sma-and-the-50-day-100-day-and-8-week)
- **10-year Treasury yield** (DGS10, TVC:US10Y) — The annual yield on 10-year US government debt: the benchmark long-term rate behind mortgages, corporate borrowing and equity valuations. It mixes expected policy rates, expected inflation and a term premium. → [03 · The 10-year Treasury yield (DGS10, US10Y)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#the-10-year-treasury-yield-dgs10-us10y)
- **10y–2y spread** (T10Y2Y) — The 10-year yield minus the 2-year yield, in percentage points; the most quoted yield-curve measure. Below zero the curve is inverted. → [03 · 10y–2y and 10y–3m spreads (T10Y2Y, T10Y3M)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#10y2y-and-10y3m-spreads-t10y2y-t10y3m)
- **10y–3m spread** (T10Y3M) — The 10-year yield minus the 3-month bill yield. The Fed's own research prefers it: it has the better recession record and feeds the New York Fed's recession-probability model. → [03 · 10y–2y and 10y–3m spreads (T10Y2Y, T10Y3M)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#10y2y-and-10y3m-spreads-t10y2y-t10y3m)
- **20W / 21W / 50W / 200W** — Shorthand for weekly moving averages: the 20-week SMA, 21-week EMA, 50-week SMA and 200-week SMA. In bitcoin bear markets the 50W, 100W and 200W have tended to be lost in that order. → [10 · 5. Moving averages and the trend regime](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#5-moving-averages-and-the-trend-regime)
- **30-year Treasury yield** (DGS30, TVC:US30Y) — The yield on 30-year US government debt; the part of the curve most exposed to fiscal worries and the term premium. → [03 · The 30-year yield and the long end (DGS30)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#the-30-year-yield-and-the-long-end-dgs30)
- **50-week SMA** (50W) — The average of the last 50 weekly closes. In Cowen's framework a bear-market rally to the 50W that fails "confirms" the bear; two weekly closes above it were his July 2026 condition to drop the bear view. → [10 · 50-week SMA](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#50-week-sma)
- **100-week and 300-week SMA** (100W, 300W) — Longer weekly moving averages used alongside the 200-week SMA to frame deep bear-market support for bitcoin. → [10 · 200-week SMA (and the 100-week and 300-week)](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#200-week-sma-and-the-100-week-and-300-week)
- **180-day realized volatility** — The standard deviation of daily returns over the last 180 days, annualized. Cowen reads very low volatility near cycle highs as "fragility", not stability. → [10 · 180-day realized volatility](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#180-day-realized-volatility)
- **200-day SMA** (200D) — The average of the last 200 daily closes; the standard dividing line between a daily uptrend and downtrend. It acted as resistance in the 2018, 2022 and 2026 bitcoin bears. → [10 · 200-day SMA (and the 50-day, 100-day and 8-week)](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#200-day-sma-and-the-50-day-100-day-and-8-week)
- **200-week SMA** (200W) — The average of the last 200 weekly closes, roughly a four-year trend line. In past bitcoin bear markets price fell to or near it; Cowen calls the meeting "a date with destiny". → [10 · 200-week SMA (and the 100-week and 300-week)](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#200-week-sma-and-the-100-week-and-300-week)
- **"300K claims"** — Cowen's 2026 rule of thumb that the US would not be in recession until weekly initial jobless claims printed around 300,000. → [05 · Initial and continuing jobless claims (ICSA, CCSA)](05-THE-LABOR-MARKET.md#initial-and-continuing-jobless-claims-icsa-ccsa)
- **1970s inflation waves** — The 1970s pattern of inflation falling, then returning in a second and larger wave after the Fed eased too early. Used as an analogy for 2021–26, with clear limits. → [04 · The 1970s "waves" analogy, and its limits](04-INFLATION.md#the-1970s-waves-analogy-and-its-limits)
- **1990s analogs** (1995, 1996–2000, 1998, 1999) — Comparisons of the current cycle with the 1990s: the 1995 soft landing, the 1998 insurance cuts and the 1999 re-hike, the 1996–2000 late-cycle melt-up. The 1995 analogy is not Cowen's. → [13 · 4.2 Soft landings and insurance cycles](13-HOW-IT-ALL-CONNECTS.md#42-soft-landings-and-insurance-cycles); [14 · 3. His recurring theses, with dates and status](14-COWEN-FRAMEWORK-AND-VIDEO-DECODER.md#3-his-recurring-theses-with-dates-and-status)
- **2019 analog** — Cowen's template for the 2025–26 cycle: a top on apathy shortly before QT ended, no alt season, then lower highs. As a bottom template it needed an outside shock (COVID in 2020). → [13 · 4.2 Soft landings and insurance cycles](13-HOW-IT-ALL-CONNECTS.md#42-soft-landings-and-insurance-cycles); [14 · 3. His recurring theses, with dates and status](14-COWEN-FRAMEWORK-AND-VIDEO-DECODER.md#3-his-recurring-theses-with-dates-and-status)
- **2026 oil shock** — The 2026 energy-price surge after US and Israeli strikes on Iran (28 Feb 2026) and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz; it pushed headline inflation up and led to the Fed's 16 Sep 2026 hike. → [04 · The 2026 oil shock and the Fed's "checkmate"](04-INFLATION.md#the-2026-oil-shock-and-the-feds-checkmate); [02 · The oil shock and the September 2026 hike](02-THE-FED-AND-INTEREST-RATES.md#the-oil-shock-and-the-september-2026-hike)

## A

- **"A date with destiny"** — Cowen's phrase for price meeting the 200-week SMA, for bitcoin or the S&P 500. → [10 · 200-week SMA (and the 100-week and 300-week)](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#200-week-sma-and-the-100-week-and-300-week)
- **A Line Has Been Crossed** — Title of Cowen's 12 Jan 2026 video on institutional risk after a reported Justice Department probe of Fed Chair Powell. → [02 · Fed chair, independence and politics](02-THE-FED-AND-INTEREST-RATES.md#fed-chair-independence-and-politics)
- **Accounting for the money supply** — Dividing an asset price by M2 (or M1) to strip out the growth of money, e.g. S&P 500 ÷ M2 or bitcoin ÷ M2. Ratios hide which side moved. → [07 · Asset ÷ M2: "accounting for the money supply"](07-LIQUIDITY-MONEY-AND-THE-DOLLAR.md#asset--m2-accounting-for-the-money-supply)
- **ACM term premium** (Adrian–Crump–Moench) — The New York Fed's daily model estimate of the term premium in Treasury yields. Model-dependent: different models give different levels. → [03 · Term premium (THREEFYTP10; NY Fed ACM)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#term-premium-threefytp10-ny-fed-acm)
- **ADP employment report** — A private-sector payroll estimate from the payroll processor ADP, released two days before the official jobs report; a supporting check, often far from the BLS figure. → [05 · Supporting labor checks: KC Fed LMCI, ADP, ISM employment](05-THE-LABOR-MARKET.md#supporting-labor-checks-kc-fed-lmci-adp-ism-employment)
- **Advance estimate** (GDP) — The first GDP release, about four weeks after the quarter ends; later estimates and annual revisions can change it materially. → [06 · Real GDP and NBER recession dating (GDPC1, A191RL1Q225SBEA, USREC)](06-GROWTH-AND-THE-BUSINESS-CYCLE.md#real-gdp-and-nber-recession-dating-gdpc1-a191rl1q225sbea-usrec)
- **Advance-decline line** (A/D) — A running total of rising minus falling stocks (or coins). It shows whether a rally is broad or carried by a few names. → [08 · Equal weight and breadth (RSP/SPY, S5TH)](08-STOCKS-GOLD-AND-CROSS-ASSET.md#equal-weight-and-breadth-rspspy-s5th)
- **AI capital-spending boom** — Large data-center and semiconductor investment by big tech from 2023; one candidate reason the 2022–24 yield-curve inversion was not followed by recession. → [03 · Why the 2022–24 inversion has not been followed by a recession (as of October 2026)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#why-the-202224-inversion-has-not-been-followed-by-a-recession-as-of-october-2026)
- **"All bitcoin pairs to range lows"; "alts to 0.25"** — Cowen's view that altcoins must capitulate against bitcoin (aggregate alt market cap near 25% of bitcoin's) before an alt season can start. → [12 · Alt/BTC pairs and the altcoin-to-BTC market-cap ratio](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#altbtc-pairs-and-the-altcoin-to-btc-market-cap-ratio)
- **All-time high** (ATH) — The highest price an asset has ever reached. Drawdowns are measured from it. → [10 · Drawdown from all-time high](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#drawdown-from-all-time-high)
- **Alt season** (altcoin season) — A period when bitcoin dominance falls while altcoins rise in dollar terms and broadly beat bitcoin. Cowen's 2026 test is a "durable rotation" that persists for months. → [12 · The Altcoin Season Index and the definition of alt season](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#the-altcoin-season-index-and-the-definition-of-alt-season)
- **Alt/BTC pairs** (e.g. BINANCE:SOLBTC) — An altcoin priced in bitcoin rather than dollars. A falling pair means the coin loses value against BTC even when its dollar price rises. → [12 · Alt/BTC pairs and the altcoin-to-BTC market-cap ratio](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#altbtc-pairs-and-the-altcoin-to-btc-market-cap-ratio)
- **Altcoin Season Index** — The share of the top coins that beat bitcoin over 90 days (blockchaincenter.net and a CoinMarketCap version). 75+ is called alt season; 25 or less bitcoin season. → [12 · The Altcoin Season Index and the definition of alt season](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#the-altcoin-season-index-and-the-definition-of-alt-season)
- **Altcoin-to-BTC market-cap ratio** — The combined market cap of all altcoins divided by bitcoin's market cap; 0.29 in October 2025 in the course data. → [12 · Alt/BTC pairs and the altcoin-to-BTC market-cap ratio](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#altbtc-pairs-and-the-altcoin-to-btc-market-cap-ratio)
- **Altcoins** (alts) — All crypto assets other than bitcoin. Higher beta and "longer duration" than BTC, so they tend to bleed against it when liquidity tightens. → [12 · 4. Why altcoins bleed against bitcoin when liquidity tightens](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#4-why-altcoins-bleed-against-bitcoin-when-liquidity-tightens)
- **"An unfortunate pattern"** — Cowen's 2026 reading of a stablecoin-dominance breakout as bearish for crypto. → [12 · Stablecoin dominance (USDT.D, USDC.D, STABLE.C.D)](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#stablecoin-dominance-usdtd-usdcd-stablecd)
- **Analog** (historical analog) — An earlier period chosen as a template for today (e.g. "the 2019 analog"). Analogs are picked after the fact, and each one ends differently. → [01 · Rebased overlays and "ROI" as a price ratio](01-HOW-TO-READ-MACRO-CHARTS.md#rebased-overlays-and-roi-as-a-price-ratio); [14 · 7. Limits and critique of each method](14-COWEN-FRAMEWORK-AND-VIDEO-DECODER.md#7-limits-and-critique-of-each-method)
- **Annualized rate** — A one-month or three-month change scaled up to a yearly pace (e.g. a 0.3% monthly CPI rise is about 3.7% annualized). Faster than YoY, but noisier. → [01 · Levels, changes and rates of change](01-HOW-TO-READ-MACRO-CHARTS.md#levels-changes-and-rates-of-change); [04 · YoY, MoM annualized and base effects](04-INFLATION.md#yoy-mom-annualized-and-base-effects)
- **Apathy top vs euphoria top** — Whether a bitcoin cycle peak came with retail mania (2017, 2021) or without it (2019, 2025). In Cowen's reading apathetic tops have been followed by no alt rotation and a choppy bear. → [11 · Apathy vs euphoria at the top](11-ON-CHAIN-AND-SENTIMENT.md#apathy-vs-euphoria-at-the-top)
- **Arthur Burns** — Fed chair 1970–78, whose early easing is the standard example of letting inflation return in a second wave. → [04 · The 1970s "waves" analogy, and its limits](04-INFLATION.md#the-1970s-waves-analogy-and-its-limits)
- **aSOPR** — Adjusted SOPR: SOPR excluding coins that moved within the last hour, to remove noise from short-lived transfers. → [11 · SOPR (Spent Output Profit Ratio)](11-ON-CHAIN-AND-SENTIMENT.md#sopr-spent-output-profit-ratio)
- **Asset ÷ M2** — See *Accounting for the money supply*. → [07 · Asset ÷ M2: "accounting for the money supply"](07-LIQUIDITY-MONEY-AND-THE-DOLLAR.md#asset--m2-accounting-for-the-money-supply)
- **Asymmetric quantile regression fan** — Cowen's 2026 model of bitcoin's price bands with separate curvature for upper and lower quantiles: the upper tail flattens across cycles while lower support stays near a power law. He calls it descriptive, not a forecast. → [10 · Asymmetric quantile regression fan (2026)](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#asymmetric-quantile-regression-fan-2026)
- **Atlanta Fed GDPNow** — See *GDPNow*. → [06 · Atlanta Fed GDPNow (GDPNOW)](06-GROWTH-AND-THE-BUSINESS-CYCLE.md#atlanta-fed-gdpnow-gdpnow)

## B

- **Balance sheet (Fed)** — See *Fed total assets*. → [07 · Fed total assets (WALCL) and the QE/QT timeline](07-LIQUIDITY-MONEY-AND-THE-DOLLAR.md#fed-total-assets-walcl-and-the-qeqt-timeline)
- **Balanced price** — An on-chain deep-value floor (David Puell): realized price minus "transferred price", an average built from coin days destroyed. Every bitcoin bear market so far has broken below it. → [11 · Balanced price, terminal price and CVDD](11-ON-CHAIN-AND-SENTIMENT.md#balanced-price-terminal-price-and-cvdd)
- **Bank of Japan** (BoJ) — Japan's central bank. Its rate hikes narrow the US–Japan rate gap and can force an unwind of the yen carry trade (e.g. 31 Jul–5 Aug 2024). → [07 · Other central banks: Bank of Japan and the People's Bank of China](07-LIQUIDITY-MONEY-AND-THE-DOLLAR.md#other-central-banks-bank-of-japan-and-the-peoples-bank-of-china)
- **Bank reserves** (WRESBAL) — Deposits commercial banks hold at the Fed. QT and a rising TGA drain them; when they become scarce, repo rates jump. → [07 · Bank reserves, SOFR and repo stress (WRESBAL, SOFR)](07-LIQUIDITY-MONEY-AND-THE-DOLLAR.md#bank-reserves-sofr-and-repo-stress-wresbal-sofr)
- **Bank Term Funding Program** (BTFP) — The Fed's 2023 emergency lending facility after Silicon Valley Bank failed; it temporarily added to the Fed's balance sheet during QT. → [07 · Fed total assets (WALCL) and the QE/QT timeline](07-LIQUIDITY-MONEY-AND-THE-DOLLAR.md#fed-total-assets-walcl-and-the-qeqt-timeline)
- **Base effect** — A year-on-year rate that moves because of what happened a year earlier, not because of anything new. A big jump 12 months ago drops out and pulls the YoY rate down. → [04 · YoY, MoM annualized and base effects](04-INFLATION.md#yoy-mom-annualized-and-base-effects); [01 · Levels, changes and rates of change](01-HOW-TO-READ-MACRO-CHARTS.md#levels-changes-and-rates-of-change)
- **Base rate** — How often an event happens at all. A signal that fires often when the event is rare produces mostly false positives. → [01 · False positives and base rates](01-HOW-TO-READ-MACRO-CHARTS.md#false-positives-and-base-rates)
- **Basis point** (bp) — One hundredth of a percentage point. A 25 bp hike raises the rate from, say, 3.75% to 4.00%. → [01 · Levels, changes and rates of change](01-HOW-TO-READ-MACRO-CHARTS.md#levels-changes-and-rates-of-change)
- **BEA** (Bureau of Economic Analysis) — The US agency that publishes GDP, GDI, personal income and the PCE price index. → [06 · Real GDP and NBER recession dating (GDPC1, A191RL1Q225SBEA, USREC)](06-GROWTH-AND-THE-BUSINESS-CYCLE.md#real-gdp-and-nber-recession-dating-gdpc1-a191rl1q225sbea-usrec)
- **Bear flattening** — The curve flattens because short yields rise faster than long ones; typical of a Fed hiking cycle. → [03 · Curve moves (bull/bear steepening and flattening)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#curve-moves-bullbear-steepening-and-flattening)
- **Bear goggles** — Cowen's term for a bearish bias; he contrasts "realists" with "doomers". → [14 · 4.6 Macro phrases](14-COWEN-FRAMEWORK-AND-VIDEO-DECODER.md#46-macro-phrases)
- **Bear market resistance band** — The bull market support band (20W SMA + 21W EMA) acting as resistance from below in a bear market. → [10 · Bear Market Resistance Band](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#bear-market-resistance-band)
- **Bear steepening** — The curve steepens because long yields rise faster than short ones; often a sign of inflation, fiscal or term-premium stress. → [03 · Curve moves (bull/bear steepening and flattening)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#curve-moves-bullbear-steepening-and-flattening)
- **Bearish divergence** — Price makes a higher high while an oscillator such as the RSI makes a lower high: momentum is fading. → [01 · Oscillators: the RSI as an example](01-HOW-TO-READ-MACRO-CHARTS.md#oscillators-the-rsi-as-an-example); [10 · RSI (daily, weekly, monthly) and divergences](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#rsi-daily-weekly-monthly-and-divergences)
- **Benford's law chart** — A niche ITC chart testing the distribution of leading digits in price data; rarely used in videos. → [10 · Niche platform charts: Cycles Deviation, Benford's law, price-milestone crossings](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#niche-platform-charts-cycles-deviation-benfords-law-price-milestone-crossings)
- **Benjamin Cowen** — Crypto and macro analyst behind the YouTube channel and platform "Into The Cryptoverse" (ITC). This course explains his vocabulary; it is not affiliated with him. → [14 · 1. How his toolset evolved](14-COWEN-FRAMEWORK-AND-VIDEO-DECODER.md#1-how-his-toolset-evolved)
- **Best day to DCA** — An ITC chart comparing average returns by weekday for regular purchases. → [14 · 8. Process and portfolio tools](14-COWEN-FRAMEWORK-AND-VIDEO-DECODER.md#8-process-and-portfolio-tools)
- **Beta** — How much an asset moves for a given move in the broader market. Altcoins have higher beta than bitcoin, small caps higher than large caps. → [12 · 4. Why altcoins bleed against bitcoin when liquidity tightens](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#4-why-altcoins-bleed-against-bitcoin-when-liquidity-tightens)
- **Bitcoin** (BTC) — The largest crypto asset, with a fixed supply schedule cut in half roughly every four years. Course data use FRED's Coinbase series CBBTCUSD. → [10 · 1. The halving clock and the four-year cycle](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#1-the-halving-clock-and-the-four-year-cycle)
- **Bitcoin dominance** (BTC.D, CRYPTOCAP:BTC.D) — Bitcoin's market cap divided by total crypto market cap. It tends to rise during QT and hikes. Stablecoins in the denominator can hide a rising BTC share, so Cowen also shows it excluding stablecoins. → [12 · Bitcoin dominance (CRYPTOCAP:BTC.D), with and without stablecoins](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#bitcoin-dominance-cryptocapbtcd-with-and-without-stablecoins)
- **Bitcoin halving** — The cut in the new-bitcoin issuance per block by half every 210,000 blocks (2012, 2016, 2020, 2024; next at block 1,050,000, estimated for about April 2028). → [10 · Bitcoin halvings (block height; mempool.space)](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#bitcoin-halvings-block-height-mempoolspace)
- **"Bitcoin is not the economy"** — Cowen's 2026 reply to the argument that an ISM above 50 means alt season: bitcoin had risen while the ISM stalled in 2025 and fell while it rose in early 2026. → [06 · ISM Manufacturing PMI: Main Street, and the alt-season debate](06-GROWTH-AND-THE-BUSINESS-CYCLE.md#ism-manufacturing-pmi-main-street-and-the-alt-season-debate)
- **Bitcoin season** — The opposite of alt season: most top coins underperform bitcoin (Altcoin Season Index at 25 or below). → [12 · The Altcoin Season Index and the definition of alt season](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#the-altcoin-season-index-and-the-definition-of-alt-season)
- **Bitcoin ÷ gold** — Bitcoin priced in ounces of gold; shows whether BTC beats the traditional store of value. Cowen's April 2026 call was a further 30–40% fall in this ratio. → [08 · Bitcoin ÷ gold](08-STOCKS-GOLD-AND-CROSS-ASSET.md#bitcoin--gold)
- **Block height** — The number of blocks in the bitcoin chain so far; halvings happen at fixed heights, so their dates are estimates until reached. → [10 · Bitcoin halvings (block height; mempool.space)](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#bitcoin-halvings-block-height-mempoolspace)
- **BLS** (Bureau of Labor Statistics) — The US agency that publishes the jobs report (payrolls, unemployment), CPI, PPI and JOLTS. → [05 · Unemployment rate, U-3 and U-6 (UNRATE, U6RATE)](05-THE-LABOR-MARKET.md#unemployment-rate-u-3-and-u-6-unrate-u6rate)
- **Bollinger Bands** — Bands set a number of standard deviations above and below a moving average; they widen when volatility rises. → [10 · MACD, Bollinger Bands, Supertrend and Heikin-Ashi candles](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#macd-bollinger-bands-supertrend-and-heikin-ashi-candles)
- **Bottom-watch mode** — A 2026 Cowen memo phrase: the framework stops confirming the decline and starts watching for the cycle low. → [13 · 11. Where we are: 30 October 2026](13-HOW-IT-ALL-CONNECTS.md#11-where-we-are-30-october-2026)
- **Breadth** — How many members of an index (or how many coins) are taking part in a move, e.g. the share above their 200-day average. Narrow breadth is a late-cycle trait. → [08 · Equal weight and breadth (RSP/SPY, S5TH)](08-STOCKS-GOLD-AND-CROSS-ASSET.md#equal-weight-and-breadth-rspspy-s5th)
- **Breakeven inflation** (T10YIE) — The nominal Treasury yield minus the TIPS real yield of the same maturity: the inflation rate the bond market is pricing. → [03 · Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) and the 5y5y forward (T5YIFR)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#breakeven-inflation-t10yie-and-the-5y5y-forward-t5yifr)
- **Brent** (DCOILBRENTEU, TVC:UKOIL) — The international crude-oil benchmark (North Sea). - **Stablecoin Supply Ratio** (SSR) — Bitcoin's market cap divided by stablecoin supply; low readings mean more stablecoin buying power relative to BTC. → [11 · Stablecoin Supply Ratio (SSR) and its oscillator](11-ON-CHAIN-AND-SENTIMENT.md#stablecoin-supply-ratio-ssr-and-its-oscillator)
- **Stagflation** — Weak growth combined with high inflation, usually after a supply shock; the hardest case for central banks. → [13 · 3.5 The supply-shock variant (stagflation risk)](13-HOW-IT-ALL-CONNECTS.md#35-the-supply-shock-variant-stagflation-risk)
- **Standing Repo Facility** (SRF) — A Fed facility that lends overnight against Treasuries at a set rate, acting as a ceiling for repo rates. → [07 · Bank reserves, SOFR and repo stress (WRESBAL, SOFR)](07-LIQUIDITY-MONEY-AND-THE-DOLLAR.md#bank-reserves-sofr-and-repo-stress-wresbal-sofr)
- **STH-MVRV** — Price divided by the short-term holders' realized price; below 1 means recent buyers are underwater on average. → [11 · Short-term and long-term holder realized price and supply (STH / LTH)](11-ON-CHAIN-AND-SENTIMENT.md#short-term-and-long-term-holder-realized-price-and-supply-sth--lth)
- **Stock-to-flow** (S2F, S2FX) — Scarcity models that price bitcoin by the ratio of existing supply to new issuance. Their forecasts failed badly out of sample. → [10 · Stock-to-flow (S2F) and why it failed](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#stock-to-flow-s2f-and-why-it-failed)
- **Summer lull** — Seasonal weakness and low volume in crypto over the summer months. → [09 · The summer lull and the yearly open](09-SEASONALITY-AND-POLITICAL-CYCLES.md#the-summer-lull-and-the-yearly-open)
- **Supercore inflation** — Core services excluding housing; a gauge of wage-driven inflation the Fed watched closely in 2022–23. → [04 · Supercore, shelter and OER](04-INFLATION.md#supercore-shelter-and-oer)
- **Supertrend** — A trend-following line that flips above or below price based on recent volatility. → [10 · MACD, Bollinger Bands, Supertrend and Heikin-Ashi candles](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#macd-bollinger-bands-supertrend-and-heikin-ashi-candles)
- **Supply in profit / supply in loss** — The share of circulating bitcoin whose last-move price is below (in profit) or above (in loss) the current price. The two lines crossing preceded past cycle lows by about 1–4 months. → [11 · Supply in profit vs supply in loss](11-ON-CHAIN-AND-SENTIMENT.md#supply-in-profit-vs-supply-in-loss)
- **Supply shock** — A jump in costs from the supply side (oil, tariffs, war) that raises inflation while slowing growth. → [04 · Oil and energy: the supply-shock channel](04-INFLATION.md#oil-and-energy-the-supply-shock-channel); [13 · 3.5 The supply-shock variant (stagflation risk)](13-HOW-IT-ALL-CONNECTS.md#35-the-supply-shock-variant-stagflation-risk)
- **Survivorship bias** — Studying only what survived (coins, funds, analysts' best calls) and ignoring what disappeared, which flatters the results. → [01 · Survivorship and curve-fitting](01-HOW-TO-READ-MACRO-CHARTS.md#survivorship-and-curve-fitting)

## T

- **Tactical vs structural rally** — Cowen's memo distinction between rallies on positioning and those backed by improving liquidity and participation. → [14 · 4.8 Terms from his written memos (2026)](14-COWEN-FRAMEWORK-AND-VIDEO-DECODER.md#48-terms-from-his-written-memos-2026)
- **Tariffs** — Import taxes. The 2025 US tariffs raised goods prices, but pass-through was modest and slow. → [04 · Tariffs, 2025](04-INFLATION.md#tariffs-2025)
- **Taylor rule** — A formula that suggests a policy rate from inflation's gap to target and the output (or unemployment) gap. Its results depend heavily on inputs. → [02 · The Taylor rule](02-THE-FED-AND-INTEREST-RATES.md#the-taylor-rule)
- **Temporary help services** (TEMPHELPS) — Employment at staffing agencies; firms cut temps first, so it has turned down before broad payrolls. → [05 · Temporary help services employment (TEMPHELPS)](05-THE-LABOR-MARKET.md#temporary-help-services-employment-temphelps)
- **Term premium** — The extra yield investors demand to hold a long bond instead of rolling short bills; it rises with inflation, fiscal and policy uncertainty. → [03 · Term premium (THREEFYTP10; NY Fed ACM)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#term-premium-threefytp10-ny-fed-acm)
- **Terminal price** — An on-chain ceiling band: "transferred price" × 21. Past bitcoin tops came near it; it was not reached in 2025. → [11 · Balanced price, terminal price and CVDD](11-ON-CHAIN-AND-SENTIMENT.md#balanced-price-terminal-price-and-cvdd)
- **Terminal rate** — The peak policy rate of a hiking cycle. Bitcoin has historically bottomed near it. → [02 · Rate hikes (Cowen: "hikes are not immediately bearish")](02-THE-FED-AND-INTEREST-RATES.md#rate-hikes-cowen-hikes-are-not-immediately-bearish); [02 · Rate cuts and "the pivot" (Cowen: "rate cuts are not bullish at first")](02-THE-FED-AND-INTEREST-RATES.md#rate-cuts-and-the-pivot-cowen-rate-cuts-are-not-bullish-at-first)
- **TGA** (Treasury General Account, WTREGEN) — The US Treasury's cash account at the Fed. When it rises (e.g. after a debt-ceiling deal), it drains reserves from the banking system. → [07 · Treasury General Account (WTREGEN)](07-LIQUIDITY-MONEY-AND-THE-DOLLAR.md#treasury-general-account-wtregen)
- **"The Fed chases the 2-year"** — The 2-year yield tends to lead the policy rate; when it falls well below fed funds, the Fed has usually cut later. → [02 · Fed funds versus the 2-year yield ("the Fed chases the 2-year")](02-THE-FED-AND-INTEREST-RATES.md#fed-funds-versus-the-2-year-yield-the-fed-chases-the-2-year)
- **Thermocap ratios** (MCTC, mCTC, RCTC) — Market cap, miner cap or realized cap divided by thermocap (the cumulative value paid to miners); valuation relative to security spending. → [11 · Thermocap ratios (MCTC, mCTC, RCTC)](11-ON-CHAIN-AND-SENTIMENT.md#thermocap-ratios-mctc-mctc-rctc)
- **Three clocks** — The course's name for the presidential cycle, the bitcoin halving cycle and the business cycle, which have tended to move in phase. → [09 · Three clocks that tick together](09-SEASONALITY-AND-POLITICAL-CYCLES.md#three-clocks-that-tick-together)
- **Three stages of a bear market** — Cowen's psychological stages: disbelief, partial acceptance, broad acceptance; dated for 2025–26 in his June 2026 video. → [10 · The three psychological stages of a bear market](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#the-three-psychological-stages-of-a-bear-market)
- **Time-based capitulation** — A bear market that resets through months of sideways drift rather than one crash; Cowen's 2026 base case without an outside shock. → [10 · Logarithmic regression bands and fair value](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#logarithmic-regression-bands-and-fair-value); [14 · 4.2 Cycles, seasonality and timing](14-COWEN-FRAMEWORK-AND-VIDEO-DECODER.md#42-cycles-seasonality-and-timing)
- **TIPS** (Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities) — Treasuries whose principal rises with CPI; their yield is the real yield. → [03 · Real yields: TIPS (DFII10)](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#real-yields-tips-dfii10)
- **TOTAL / TOTAL2 / TOTAL3** — TradingView indices of total crypto market cap, excluding BTC, and excluding BTC and ETH. → [12 · TOTAL, TOTAL2, TOTAL3, OTHERS and OTHERS.D](12-DOMINANCE-ALTCOINS-AND-ROTATION.md#total-total2-total3-others-and-othersd)
- **Total crypto market cap vs trendline** — How far the whole crypto market is above or below its regression trend; the subject of Cowen's "The Beauty of Mathematics" series. → [10 · Total crypto market cap vs its trendline ("The Beauty of Mathematics")](10-BITCOIN-CYCLES-AND-VALUATION-MODELS.md#total-crypto-market-cap-vs-its-trendline-the-beauty-of-mathematics)
- **TradingView** — A charting platform; its ticker prefixes (TVC:, CRYPTOCAP:, BINANCE:, SP:, ECONOMICS:) are used throughout the course. → [15 · 1. The dashboard](15-YOUR-DASHBOARD-AND-ROUTINE.md#1-the-dashboard)
- **Treasury yields** — The interest rates on US government debt at each maturity; together they form the yield curve. → [03 · Treasury yields along the curve](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#treasury-yields-along-the-curve)
- **Triple squeeze** — Cowen's September 2026 memo theme: rising long yields, a firming dollar and rising oil tightening conditions together. → [03 · The "triple squeeze" and its loop](03-BONDS-AND-THE-YIELD-CURVE.md#the-triple-squeeze-and-its-loop)

## U

- **U-3 / U-6** (UNRATE, U6RATE) — U-3 is the official unemployment rate. U-6 adds discouraged and marginally attached workers and part-timers who want full-time work. → [05 · Unemployment rate, U-3 and U-6 (UNRATE, U6RATE)](05-THE-LABOR-MARKET.md#unemployment-rate-u-3-and-u-6-unrate-u6rate)
- **Un-inversion** — The yield curve moving back above zero after an inversion, usually because the Fed starts cutting. - **Unemployment composition** — The breakdown of the unemployed into job losers, leavers, re-entrants and new entrants; a rise led by job losers is more worrying. → [05 · Unemployment composition: job losers, entrants and youth](05-THE-LABOR-MARKET.md#unemployment-composition-job-losers-entrants-and-youth)
- **Uptober** — Crypto slang for October's historically strong bitcoin returns. → [09 · Q4: "Uptober", post-halving Q4 tops and midterm Q4 drops](09-SEASONALITY-AND-POLITICAL-CYCLES.md#q4-uptober-post-halving-q4-tops-and-midterm-q4-drops)

- **USREC** — FRED's monthly flag that equals 1 during NBER-dated recessions; used by the lab to date recessions. → [06 · Real GDP and NBER recession dating (GDPC1, A191RL1Q225SBEA, USREC)](06-GROWTH-AND-THE-BUSINESS-CYCLE.md#real-gdp-and-nber-recession-dating-gdpc1-a191rl1q225sbea-usrec)
